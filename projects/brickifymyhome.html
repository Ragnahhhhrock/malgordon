Brickify My Home

2026 — Present · Founder

Turn your house plans, CAD files or photos into a custom, LEGO-compatible brick model of your own home — complete with a 3D preview and a step-by-step instruction book. Currently in early access.

Visit Brickify My Home →

Outcomes

  • Building a pipeline that converts house plans, CAD files or reference photos into a buildable, LEGO-compatible brick model of a customer's own home.
  • Designed an interactive 3D preview so customers can see and rotate their model before ordering.
  • Each order ships with its own printed instruction book, so the model can be built brick by brick at home.
  • Extended the concept beyond houses to landmark builds (e.g. the Eiffel Tower) and gift cards, broadening the product beyond a single use case.
  • Opened an early-access waitlist ahead of the first customer shipments.
