Read My Spread

2026 — Present · Founder

Domain registered: 8 Oct 2026

Photograph your tarot spread and get a clear reading: it identifies the layout and each card, then explains every position in plain language. Free, with no account required.

Visit Read My Spread →

Explainer video

Outcomes

  • Shipped a photo-to-reading tool: upload a picture of a tarot spread and it identifies the layout and every card, including reversed cards.
  • Recognises common spreads — single card, three-card, Celtic Cross and horseshoe — and describes freeform layouts.
  • Returns a summary of the whole spread followed by a short reading for each card based on its position, with an optional question to focus the reading.
  • Flags cards it cannot identify confidently rather than guessing, and works with most standard 78-card decks.
  • Designed for privacy: no account required, and photos, questions and readings are not stored.
