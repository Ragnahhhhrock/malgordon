Pumpkin Smash

2026 — Present · Founder

Launch date: 28 Oct 2026

A free, mobile-first Halloween tap game. Pumpkins pop up and hide again; tap them before they disappear, chase golden pumpkins for bonus points, catch ghosts for extra lives and avoid the black cats.

Visit Pumpkin Smash →

Outcomes

  • Shipped a free, mobile-first Halloween tap game in time for Halloween 2026.
  • Four target types with distinct rules: pumpkin (+1 point), golden pumpkin (+5 points), ghost (+1 life) and black cat (penalty — leave it alone).
  • Added a worldwide "pumpkins smashed" counter and a high-score table to drive repeat play.
