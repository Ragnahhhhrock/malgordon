SetsOfLove

2026 — Present · Founder

Domain registered: 7 Oct 2026

A profile site for single gym-goers. Members build a profile around their training photos and optional self-reported lifts, every profile is reviewed before it goes live, and each member gets a personal link to share. Invitation-only, for adults of every gender.

Visit SetsOfLove →

Explainer video

Outcomes

  • Shipped a profile builder that puts gym photos first: one to four photos, with a clear face shot as the main image.
  • Optional self-reported lifts (bench, squat, deadlift, overhead press) entered in kg or lb and displayed in both units.
  • Manual moderation: every profile is approved before going live and re-checked after each edit, with a report link on every profile.
  • No comments, likes or messages by design — members share a personal link (e.g. setsoflove.com/sam-t) instead.
  • Quick-pick suggestions under each field so a full profile takes about 10 minutes.
