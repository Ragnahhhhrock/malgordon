Stuck in Line

2026 — Present · Founder

Domain registered: 6 Oct 2026

A game about waiting. Every player joins one continuous global queue at the back of the line — and nobody knows what's at the front.

Visit Stuck in Line →

Outcomes

  • Shipped a real-time multiplayer game built around a single, continuous global queue that every player joins at the back.
  • Designed a touch-first interface: drag to look around, pinch to zoom, tap to focus on other players, and slide a rail to walk the line.
  • Built a "Find me" function so each player can locate their own place in the queue, plus a jump to the front.
  • Set the scene in a dark, night-time street with a single-file queue leading to a glowing theatre curtain, keeping the mystery of what waits at the front.
