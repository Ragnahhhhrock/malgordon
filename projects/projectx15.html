Project X-15

2026 — Present · Founder

Launch date: 27 Oct 2026

A browser-based flight simulator of the North American X-15 rocket plane, set at Edwards AFB during the 1959–1968 flight programme — drop from the NB-52, light the XLR99, ride it to the edge of space and land on Rogers Dry Lake.

Visit Project X-15 →

Outcomes

  • Shipped a browser flight simulator covering the full X-15 mission profile: air launch from the NB-52 carrier, XLR99 rocket burn, ballistic climb, re-entry and unpowered landing.
  • References the programme's record figures: Mach 6.72 top speed, 354,200 ft peak altitude and an approximately 85-second engine burn.
  • Set the landing on Rogers Dry Lake at Edwards AFB, matching the historical recovery site.
