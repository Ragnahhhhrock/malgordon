PowerPoint2Vid

2026 — Present · Founder

A browser-based converter that turns PowerPoint (.pptx) and Google Slides decks into narrated, animated videos. Speaker notes become narration, and slides are processed on the user's device without being uploaded.

Visit PowerPoint2Vid →

Outcomes

  • Converts .pptx files, including decks exported from Google Slides, into narrated videos.
  • Generates narration from speaker notes, with slide text as a fallback, using the browser's built-in text-to-speech voices.
  • Adds animated shape reveals, image drift, slide transitions and sentence-by-sentence burned-in captions.
  • Runs parsing, narration and recording entirely on the user's device, so slides are never uploaded.
  • Free preview, with video export (WebM or MP4) available on a paid Pro plan.
