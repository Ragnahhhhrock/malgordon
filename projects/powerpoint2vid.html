PowerPoint2Vid

2026 — Present · Founder

A PowerPoint to video converter that turns .pptx and Google Slides decks into narrated explainer videos. A free Basic tier converts decks in the browser from speaker notes; the Pro tier uses AI to restructure the story and redesign every slide as an animated scene.

Visit PowerPoint2Vid →

Outcomes

  • Operates as a freemium SaaS product with two tiers: a free Basic converter and a Pro subscription at A$29 per month that includes 10 AI-directed videos.
  • Pro uses AI to tighten the narrative and redesign each slide as an animated scene, timed to the narration.
  • Pro rebuilds slide charts as animated charts synchronised with the voiceover.
  • Basic runs in the browser: narration from speaker notes (slide text as fallback) via built-in text-to-speech, six video styles, animated reveals, transitions and burned-in captions, with no slide limit.
  • Exports WebM or MP4 video; Basic exports carry a watermark, Pro exports do not.
  • Handles accounts and recurring billing through Stripe Checkout, supporting cards, Apple Pay, Link and Klarna.
  • Supports organic acquisition with a product showcase, a blog and a pricing page benchmarked against Synthesia, HeyGen and Pictory.
