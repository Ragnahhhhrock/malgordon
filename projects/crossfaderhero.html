Crossfader Hero

2026 — Present · Founder

Domain registered: 12 Oct 2026

A free browser-based DJ game. Players load their own MP3s onto two virtual decks and a mixer in a 3D bedroom, learn to beatmatch, and progress from bedroom practice to headlining a festival. No install required.

Visit Crossfader Hero →

Outcomes

  • Built a 3D bedroom DJ setup with two decks, a mixer and speakers, rendered in the browser.
  • Players load their own MP3 files onto the decks, so every mix uses their own music.
  • Beatmatching practice teaches the fundamentals of tempo and phrase alignment between two tracks.
  • Career progression takes players from bedroom practice through to a festival main stage.
  • Free to play, with no install or account required.
