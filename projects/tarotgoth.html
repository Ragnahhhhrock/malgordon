Tarot Goth

2026 — Present · Founder

Domain registered: 8 Oct 2026

Photograph your tarot spread and get a reading from Vesper, a dark and sarcastic reader with a gothic sense of humour. The site identifies the spread and each card, then delivers the verdict. Your cards, her opinion.

Visit Tarot Goth →

Outcomes

  • Shipped a photo-to-reading tool: take a photo of a tarot spread, or choose one from the camera roll, and it identifies the spread and each card.
  • Wrote a distinct reader persona, Vesper, whose sardonic gothic tone sets the product apart from standard tarot apps.
  • Guides users to a usable photo: whole spread in frame, good lighting, cards laid flat.
  • Designed for privacy: photos and readings are not saved, and the site states it is for entertainment only.
